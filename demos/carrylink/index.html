How it works

Four steps. Real income.

Step 01

Design your card

Pick a template, drop in your name, drag elements where you want them. Export to PNG anytime.

Step 02

Get a unique short link (crly.to/yourcode). Custom codes unlock at Silver tier.

Step 03

Hand it out

Print it, AirDrop it, paste the link in your email signature. Every scan is attributed to you.

Get paid

10–22% of every conversion lands in your account. Weekly payouts, no minimums.
